Midnight's Terran Research  (v1.05, X4: Foundations 9.00+)
by Midnight

WHAT IT DOES
I play Terran, and it always bugged me that my Player HQ research asked for claytronics and
hull parts. This mod makes HQ research cost Terran wares instead.

I didn't want it to be a free ride, so every research still costs about the same in credits
as before (within 1%). Small research is mostly energy cells, ore, silicon and microlattice;
the big ones lean on silicon carbide and computronic substrate.

It covers 34 research items: the base game, plus the Envoy Pack, Tides of Avarice and
Timelines DLCs if you have them.

It only affects you: Player HQ research is something only the player does, so NPC factions are
untouched.

REQUIREMENTS
  - X4: Foundations 9.00 or later
  - Cradle of Humanity DLC (the Terran wares only exist with it)

Using X4 Upgrade Project? Grab the optional add-on "Midnight's Terran Research - XUP
Compatibility" as well, and XUP's research gets the same treatment.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Safe to add or remove mid-game.
  - Don't run it with another mod that changes the same research costs.

INSTALL
Subscribe on the Steam Workshop, or copy the folder into  X4 Foundations/extensions/  so you
have  extensions/midnights_terran_research/content.xml
